Frequently asked questions

For Hub users

Getting started

How do I register at the Hub?

Tap “Create account”, fill in your details (RUT with a hyphen, date of birth, email) and accept the terms. You’ll get a code by email to activate your account. You only register once; you can also do it at reception.

How do I enter and leave the Hub?

Show the QR code on Home at reception when you come in and when you leave. If you don’t have your phone, reception can find you by your RUT.

I forgot my password

On the sign-in screen, type your email, tap “Send code to my email” and use the code you receive (valid for 15 minutes). Then change it in Profile › Settings.

Do I need to book the cowork area?

No. The cowork area is free to use: come in with your QR and use any free desk.

Minors

How do I register a minor?

The minor creates an account like anyone else. When entering their date of birth, the system asks for the ID or RUT (or QR) of an adult with a Hub profile. The adult gets a notice on their Home and must tap “Yes, I authorise”. From then on, the minor’s profile is active.

What does being the responsible adult mean?

Whoever authorises becomes responsible for the minor’s stay at the Hub, including accidents, lost equipment or misconduct. If you don’t want to take that on, tap “No”.

Is there a minimum age?

Yes. Registration accepts people from 4 to 95 years old.

Bookings

How do I make a booking?

Go to Book, choose the type of space, the day and one or more consecutive blocks. If others are coming with you, add them by their ID or RUT. Tap “Confirm booking”. You’ll see it in My bookings and on Home.

How do I start my booking?

First check in to the Hub at reception. Then, on Home, tap “Start your booking” and scan the QR at the entrance of the space (or type the code shown under it). You can do it from 10 minutes before. If you don’t confirm within 15 minutes, the booking is released. You can also ask reception to start it.

How do I finish my booking?

Tap “Finish” under your QR and go to reception within 5 minutes so they can scan your QR. That records your exit from the lab, charges the actual time until you tapped “Finish” and moves you to the cowork area. If you don’t get to reception in time, the finish doesn’t count: your booking stays in progress and you get a notice. If you leave the Hub without tapping “Finish”, scanning your QR at reception records your exit and ends your booking at that moment.

How many bookings can I have?

Up to 3 active bookings at a time. Rooms and the gaming room are booked in 50-minute blocks with a 10-minute break.

How do I cancel a booking?

In My bookings, tap “Cancel booking”. If you cancel at least 15 minutes in advance, you get your hours back.

What if I’m late?

You have 15 minutes of grace. After that, the booking is released for someone else.

Labs

How do I request a certification?

Go to Book › Certifications, choose the lab and tap “Enrol” in a session with seats left. You can have one session per certification. Attend the session; the lab manager records the result and, if you pass, you can book that lab.

The session says “Full”

There are no seats left in that session. Choose another date; a new one opens every week.

How do I book a lab?

With your certification approved, go to Book › Labs, upload your work file, enter how long it takes and choose the time. The lab manager reviews the file and the booking is confirmed.

What are my monthly hours?

Each lab gives you a monthly balance of hours. It resets on the 1st and doesn’t carry over. When a job finishes, the actual time is deducted.

A machine is not working

In your booking, tap “Report a fault”. The notice goes to the lab manager.

How do I download my certificate?

In Profile, under Certifications, tap “Download certificate” next to the certification. It downloads as a PDF.

Events

How do I sign up for an event?

Tap the banner on Home or go to Book › Events and tap “Sign up”. On the day of the event, check in from Home.

The banners move on their own

They change every 5 seconds. If you swipe one or use the arrows, they stop. The pause button turns them back on.

Account and data

Do I have to sign in every time?

No. When signing in, keep “Keep me signed in on this device” ticked. The session stays open on that phone or computer until you tap “Sign out” or change your password. Profile shows whether your session is saved and lets you sign out on your other devices.

What does the Hub do with my data?

It uses them to record entries and exits, manage bookings and produce statistics without your name or RUT. They are not shared with third parties. In Profile › Settings › Privacy and my data you can ask for a copy or for your account to be deleted.

My access says “restricted”

You have an active penalty. Home shows until when and why. If you think it’s a mistake, talk to reception.

Internship

How do I enter the internship office?

If the Hub assigned you an internship, Home shows the “Enter the internship office” button. Check in at reception, tap the button and scan the office’s QR. When you leave, tap “Leave the internship office”. If the office is full, wait for someone to leave.
